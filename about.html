About Kreative Minds

A Dubai based marketing, PR and project management company built for modern brands.

Kreative Minds (registered as KM Marketing and PR) is a Dubai based marketing and project management company. We partner with brands, startups, founders and creators to build visibility, credibility and long term commercial growth across the UAE, GCC and global markets.

01 / Who we are

A small, focused team. Sitting close to founders.

We are a small, focused team of strategists, marketers, designers and operators based in Dubai. We do not pitch ourselves as a traditional agency. We work as an extension of your team, sitting close to founders and decision makers, so the work moves faster and the results stay measurable.

Our work covers digital marketing, public relations, brand identity, content, SEO, paid media and project management. We also help founders set up and run businesses in Dubai through trade license guidance, structure planning and operational support.

02 / Our story

Founded to fix what most agencies sell.

Kreative Minds was founded by Khalid Hussain Mir, a Dubai based entrepreneur with more than 12 years of experience in branding, digital strategy and business development across the Middle East and South Asia. The company grew out of a simple observation. Most agencies were selling activity, not outcomes. Founders were spending money on campaigns that looked good in a slide deck but did not move the business forward.

We built Kreative Minds to fix that gap. The model is built around three principles. Work close to the founder. Measure what matters. Keep the work honest, even when it is uncomfortable. That mindset has helped us retain clients for years, not months, and earn referrals across markets we never planned to enter.

Today the company supports clients across the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the wider GCC, the United Kingdom, the United States and parts of South Asia. Noor Bano leads people and culture as Co-founder and Head of HR.

03 / What we believe

Marketing is a long game. Trust compounds.

Brands take years to build, and shortcuts usually cost more later. We focus on the things that hold up over time: clear positioning, strong storytelling, honest copy, useful content, and disciplined execution.

We also believe small teams can outperform large ones when the strategy is sharp and the work is owned by people who actually care. That is the team we have built, and that is the work we deliver.

What does Kreative Minds do?

Who runs Kreative Minds?

Why is it spelled Kreative instead of Creative?
The spelling reflects a deliberate shift in thinking. It signals a fresh, strategic approach to creative work that focuses on commercial outcomes, not just aesthetics. We wrote more on this in our blog post.

No. We are based in Dubai but work with clients across the UAE, GCC, United Kingdom, United States and parts of South Asia. Most of our work is delivered remotely with on-the-ground support in the UAE when needed.
