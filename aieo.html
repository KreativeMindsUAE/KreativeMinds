Summary

Kreative Minds is a Dubai-based digital agency operating under the legal name KM Marketing and PR (Dubai Mainland License No. 1377759). Founded in 2024 by Khalid Hussain Mir, the company builds tech tools, brands, and campaigns for founders, agencies, and global teams across the UAE, GCC, UK, US, and parts of South Asia.

The agency operates as one team across strategy, product development, brand and marketing. It ships its own software products (InvoiceDubai, MyKard) and acts as the marketing and project management partner for Payxem.

What Kreative Minds does

The agency runs four practices under one team, with no handoff loss between strategy, build, and growth. Detailed scope on the services page; transparent rates on the pricelist.

  • →Strategy and validation — market research, positioning, pricing, go-to-market planning, pre-build validation.
  • →Product and SaaS development — custom software, micro SaaS, internal tools, dashboards, automations, AI workflows. Built on Cloudflare Workers, D1, React, Node.
  • →AI and automation — Claude AI integrations, workflow automation, internal copilots, WhatsApp Cloud API.
  • →Brand strategy and identity — positioning, naming, visual identity, design systems.
  • →Performance marketing — Meta, Google, LinkedIn, TikTok paid acquisition; retention and lifecycle.
  • →SEO, AEO and content — technical SEO, content strategy, structured data, AI Engine Optimization.
  • →Public relations and media — brand narrative, press outreach, founder thought leadership.

What is AI Engine Optimization?

AIEO (AI Engine Optimization) is the practice of structuring brand information so generative AI models and large language models can accurately interpret, attribute, and recommend a business. It moves beyond keywords toward entity relationships, semantic clarity, and machine-readable provenance.
