Building InvoiceDubai: free VAT invoices and payslips in the browser

A UAE freelancer should not need to buy accounting software just to send one compliant invoice. InvoiceDubai is our answer: a proper VAT invoice, free, in the browser, in under a minute.

Published July 18, 2026 6 min read

Some of the most valuable things you can build are small and free. InvoiceDubai does one job, does it well, and asks for nothing in return.

The problem

Freelancers and small businesses in the UAE regularly need two documents: a VAT-compliant tax invoice and a clean salary payslip. The usual options are heavy accounting suites with monthly fees, or fiddly spreadsheets that get the format wrong. For someone who just needs to send one correct invoice this week, both are overkill.

The brief was to make generating a correct UAE tax invoice, and a MOHRE-style payslip, as quick and painless as possible, and to make it free.

What we built

InvoiceDubai is a focused, browser-based generator.

  • UAE tax invoices. Auto-calculated 5 percent VAT, a TRN field, and multi-currency with AED as the default.
  • Salary payslips. MOHRE-style payslips with allowances and deductions.
  • Clean PDF export. Documents download as PDFs kept under 500KB, so they attach and send easily.
  • Branding. Logo upload and customisation so the document looks like it came from a real business.
  • Sequential numbering. Invoice numbers that increment and can reset monthly.
  • Works offline, no account needed. Generation and download happen in the browser. An optional free account lets you save, brand, and send, but nothing is gated behind sign-up.

For the wider context on the rules these documents follow, we have separate guides on UAE e-invoicing and UAE corporate tax for SMBs and freelancers.

A free tool that genuinely helps is one of the best marketing assets a business can own.

The approach and the stack

The defining decision was to do the work in the browser. Generating the document client-side means it is fast, it works offline, and the user's data does not need to leave their device for the core task. That is a better experience and a simpler, cheaper thing to run. An optional lightweight backend handles saving and sending for those who want an account, but the tool is useful the moment it loads, with zero friction.

Decisions that mattered

  • Free, and free properly. The core is not a trial. Generation and download work forever without paying or signing up.
  • No account wall. Asking someone to register before they can get value is the fastest way to lose them.
  • UAE-specific by design. The 5 percent VAT, TRN field, and AED default are built in, so the output fits the local format. For any specific tax obligation, users should still confirm with a qualified advisor.
  • Small, sharp, shareable. One job done well beats a bloated suite nobody finishes setting up.

What it demonstrates

InvoiceDubai shows the value of a well-built free tool as a growth asset. It is genuinely useful, it ranks and gets shared, and it introduces people to Kreative Minds without a sales pitch. Building focused tools like this, fast, free, and on-brand, is a service in its own right, and part of how we think about product and growth together rather than separately.
