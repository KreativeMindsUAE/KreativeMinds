eCommerce business setup in Dubai: license, marketplace, and stock.

A 2026 guide to launching an online business in Dubai the practical way: the free zone license that fits online sellers, how to set up and run Amazon.ae and Noon stores, and how to source stock without locking up your cash.

Published August 8, 2026 7 min read

Search demand for eCommerce in Dubai keeps climbing, and most of it comes from founders asking the same thing: what is the shortest clear path from company to first sale? This guide gives you that path in 2026. It covers the license that suits an online business, how to launch on Amazon.ae and Noon, and where to source stock so your cash is not stuck in a warehouse.

Start with a free zone eCommerce license

For an online-first business selling across the UAE and the wider GCC, a free zone eCommerce license is usually the better starting point. It costs less to set up, gives you full ownership, issues quickly, and carries an activity that explicitly covers online trading. A mainland license earns its extra cost later, once you open a physical store or need to bill UAE government and corporate clients directly. If you are weighing the two, our breakdown of Dubai free zone vs mainland in 2026 walks through the trade-offs, and our step-by-step guide to starting an eCommerce business in Dubai covers the wider setup.

Whichever route you pick, you will still need a corporate bank account, and once your revenue crosses the VAT threshold, VAT registration and clean invoicing become mandatory.

Decide where you sell first: Amazon.ae, Noon, or your own store

Most sellers start on a marketplace because the traffic and trust already exist. Amazon.ae and Noon both put your product in front of active UAE buyers on day one. Your own store gives you better margin and full customer data, but you have to earn the traffic yourself. A common 2026 play is to launch on one marketplace to prove demand, then add your own website once the numbers make sense. Our comparison of Noon vs Amazon vs your own store helps you choose by product category and stage.

Source stock without locking up your cash

Sourcing decides your margin and your risk. You can import directly, buy from local distributors, or work with a B2B wholesale partner inside the UAE so stock ships fast and clears customs cleanly. FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need to live in the UAE to sell on Amazon.ae or Noon?

No. With a free zone company you can register and sell as a non-resident. You do not need a UAE residence visa to open a seller account, though you do need a valid trade license and a way to receive payouts.

Is a free zone or mainland license better for eCommerce?

For an online-first business selling on marketplaces or your own website, a free zone eCommerce license is usually the better fit: cheaper to set up, full ownership, and quick to issue. Mainland makes more sense once you open a physical store or bill UAE clients directly.

How much does it cost to set up an eCommerce business in Dubai?

Costs vary by free zone and activity, but plan for the license, a corporate bank account, and your first batch of stock. Keeping the first order small and reordering what sells protects your cash while you validate demand.

Where do I source stock for a Dubai online store?

Import directly, buy from local distributors, or use a B2B wholesale partner inside the UAE so stock ships fast and clears customs. We introduce sellers to sourcing partners like GulfWholesale, plus other suppliers, so you can start with tested products.
