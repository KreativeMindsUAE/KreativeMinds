KM Contribution

Selecting Impact

We support a portfolio of emerging technologies that remove CO₂ permanently.

Climeworks

Direct Air Capture

Climeworks uses renewable geothermal energy to capture CO₂ directly from the air and permanently sequester it in basaltic rock formations. This permanent solution scales as early adopters fund the cost-curve decline.

Charm Industrial

Geologic Storage

Charm converts plant waste into bio-oil and injects it deep underground for permanent storage. Stripe Climate was Charm’s first customer, proving the power of early adoption in building the future.

Expert Selection through Frontier

No company can stop climate change alone. Stripe Climate aggregates funds from forward-thinking businesses and works with Frontier—a team of scientists and commercial experts—to identify and purchase permanent carbon removal.
