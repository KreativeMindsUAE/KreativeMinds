Why Carbon Removal?

Reducing emissions is essential, but it isn’t enough. To counteract climate change, we must also remove CO₂ directly from the atmosphere.

A fraction of every purchase from Kreative Minds helps new technologies scale. Most new technology is expensive at first; early adopters like us help promising companies lower their costs and scale up quickly.
