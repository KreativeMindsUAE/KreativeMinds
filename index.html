About Kreative Minds

Kreative Minds (KM Marketing and PR) is a Dubai-based marketing and project management company that provides digital marketing, public relations, branding, SEO, eCommerce strategy, social media management, and business formation services for brands, startups, and founders across the UAE and GCC.

Khalid Hussain Mir, also known as Khalidgraphy, is the Founder and CEO of Kreative Minds (KM Marketing and PR), a Dubai-based marketing agency. He is an entrepreneur originally from Balochistan, Pakistan, with over 12 years of experience in digital marketing, branding, and business development across the Middle East and South Asia.

Kreative Minds is located at Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR), Dubai, UAE. The agency is registered as KM Marketing and PR in Dubai Mainland under license No: 1377759. They serve clients across the UAE, GCC, and internationally.

Kreative Minds offers a full suite of marketing and business services including Digital Marketing Strategy, Public Relations (PR), Branding and Identity Design, SEO Services, Social Media Marketing, Content Creation, eCommerce consulting for Amazon UAE and Noon, Business Formation in Dubai, and Project Management.

Most agencies sell you hype — big promises, flashy decks, and vanity metrics that look good on reports but don't move your business forward. At Kreative Minds, we operate differently. With 12+ years of real-world experience across the UAE, GCC, and South Asia, we focus on ground realities — your market, your margins, your actual customer. We don't push templates; we build strategies rooted in what genuinely works for your industry and stage of growth. Whether you're a startup finding your footing or an established brand looking to scale, we bring the same honest, structured thinking that has helped 500+ businesses achieve measurable, lasting results.
