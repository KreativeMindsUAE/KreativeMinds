03 / FAQ

Common questions.

Does Kreative Minds hire full-time permanent staff?

No. We work with remote, contract based freelancers and independent specialists. We do not offer full-time permanent employment.

Are the roles remote?

Yes. Every role is remote. Most of our team delivers from across South Asia while our clients sit in the UAE, GCC, UK, and Europe.

Which locations do you hire freelancers from?

We work with freelancers across the UAE, GCC, and South Asia, including Pakistan and India. Location is flexible because the work is remote.

How do I apply for a freelance role?

Send your resume or portfolio to hr@kmmpr.com with links to previous work and a short intro about your experience. We respond to every application within a working week.
