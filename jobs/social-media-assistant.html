Social Media Assistant

Remote Meta Ads Future UAE relocation opportunity

Kreative Minds is a Dubai based marketing and project management agency working with businesses and brands across the UAE and international markets. We are looking for a Social Media Assistant to support both our internal brands and, most importantly, our client projects.

This is a remote position initially, with the possibility of relocation to our UAE office and visa support for candidates who demonstrate strong performance, reliability, and growth potential.

The role

You will work closely with our marketing and project management team to manage social media activities for multiple brands and client accounts. The role combines day-to-day social media management with hands-on support for digital marketing campaigns.

Responsibilities

  • Manage social media accounts for Kreative Minds and our clients
  • Plan, schedule, publish, and monitor content across social platforms
  • Assist with content calendars and campaign planning
  • Manage Meta Business Suite and support Facebook and Instagram campaigns
  • Set up, monitor, and optimize Meta Ads campaigns
  • Track ad performance, budgets, leads, and key campaign metrics
  • Prepare simple campaign and social media performance reports
  • Monitor audience engagement, comments, messages, and leads
  • Research trends, competitors, audiences, and content opportunities
  • Coordinate with designers, content creators, clients, and project managers
  • Maintain organized records of campaigns, creatives, budgets, and results
  • Support other digital marketing tasks as required

What we are looking for

  • Practical experience managing social media pages or client accounts
  • Hands-on Meta Ads experience is strongly preferred
  • Familiarity with Meta Ads Manager and Meta Business Suite
  • Understanding of campaign objectives, targeting, audiences, budgets, and basic optimization
  • Good English communication and reporting skills
  • Strong attention to detail and ability to manage multiple projects
  • Comfortable working with deadlines and remote teams
  • Agency, freelance, or client management experience is a strong advantage
  • Basic knowledge of Canva or other content tools is a plus

Preferred candidates

We welcome applications globally, with preference for candidates based in Pakistan, India, Bangladesh, the Philippines, and other South Asian markets.

What we offer

  • Remote work initially
  • Opportunity to work on real UAE and international client projects
  • Exposure to multiple industries and marketing campaigns
  • Performance based growth opportunities
  • Possibility of relocation to our Dubai office
  • UAE employment visa support for selected candidates based on experience and performance
  • Opportunity to grow with a Dubai based marketing and project management agency

How to apply

Send your application to hr@kmmpr.com and include the following:

  1. Updated CV or resume
  2. Portfolio or examples of your social media work
  3. Meta Business Portfolio or business profile link, if available
  4. Previous Meta Ads campaign experience
  5. Approximate total ad spend managed, if available
  6. Examples of campaigns, results, leads, or other measurable outcomes
  7. Brief introduction about yourself and your availability

Important: We are looking for someone with genuine hands-on experience. If you have managed Meta Ads for clients or businesses, please provide verifiable examples or campaign history where possible.

Ready to apply?

Send your CV, portfolio, and Meta Ads experience to our HR team. We respond to every application within a working week.
