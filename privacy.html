Privacy & Cookie Policy

Effective Date: 24 May 2024 | Last Updated: 24 February 2026

Kreative Minds operates under KM Marketing and PR, a company licensed in Dubai Mainland, United Arab Emirates. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit kreativeminds.ae or engage our services.

Personal Information

  • Name

  • Phone number
  • Company name
  • Project details submitted through our contact forms

Technical Information

  • IP address

  • Pages visited
  • Date and time of access

We collect this information when you submit a form, contact us directly, or interact with our website.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable, we process personal data based on:

Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website may use essential cookies and basic analytics tools to ensure proper functionality and understand general visitor behavior.

Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help improve website performance and user experience.

We may use analytics services to collect non-sensitive usage data such as page visits and traffic patterns. This information is used only for performance improvement and business insights.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.

You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

Where services such as business formation coordination or international support are involved, information may be shared with licensed partner firms as necessary to deliver agreed services.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that third parties handle data securely.

As a Dubai based firm working with remote teams and international partners, your information may be processed outside your country of residence. We apply appropriate safeguards to protect your data.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill business purposes or comply with legal obligations.

Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. However, no online transmission method can be guaranteed as fully secure.
