Standards & Ethics.

01 / About

What we do and how we do it.

Kreative Minds, operating under KM Marketing and PR, is a Dubai-based digital agency with a valid Dubai Mainland professional license. We help entrepreneurs, startups, and growing businesses build sustainable brands and ship the software that powers them.

Our services include:

  • Strategy and business planning
  • Brand development and identity
  • Custom software, SaaS, and AI automation
  • Marketing strategy and execution
  • Digital marketing, SEO, and online presence
  • Website and digital experience design
  • Social media and content production
  • Project management for business initiatives
  • Business formation across UAE and globally
02 / Mission & Values

Long-term partnerships, not transactions.

To help businesses grow through smart strategy, honest marketing, and reliable execution. We build long-term strategic partnerships rather than short-term transactions.

Transparency

Clear communication about scope, costs, and expected outcomes. No hidden retainers.

Honesty in Marketing

Responsible, truthful campaigns. We don't promise outcomes we can't deliver.

Quality Over Volume

Proper project execution over chasing client count. We take on what we can ship well.

Respect & Inclusion

Treating all partners and clients with professionalism regardless of background.

03 / Ethical business policy

Who we work with, who we don't.

Our commitment

Kreative Minds evaluates potential engagements based on nature and impact. We provide consulting, marketing, and product services to professionals with legitimate commercial objectives.

This policy helps maintain neutrality, professional integrity, and responsible use of marketing and technical practices.

Services we do not provide

Religious organisations Faith-based institutions seeking fundraising or PR support.
Political entities Political parties, candidates, or campaigns of any type.
Advocacy groups Entities whose primary purpose is religious or political influence.
Discrimination Organisations promoting intolerance or racial superiority.
